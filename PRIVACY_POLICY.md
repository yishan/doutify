# Privacy Policy for Doutify

**Last Updated:** December 09, 2025

## Introduction
Doutify ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome extension handles your information.

## Data Collection and Use
**We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data.**

Doutify operates strictly as a client-side tool. All processing happens locally on your device or directly between your browser and the Douban Music website (`https://music.douban.com/`). We do not have any servers that receive your data, nor do we use any third-party analytics or tracking services.

## Permissions Usage
We request the minimum set of permissions necessary for Doutify to function:

- **`storage`**: Used to save your local preferences (such as your client ID/secret configuration) directly in your browser. This data is never sent to us.
- **`activeTab` / Host Permissions (`https://music.douban.com/*`)**: Used solely to interact with the Douban Music "New Subject" page to autofill album information. This interaction is local to your browser session.

## Third-Party Services
This extension interacts with:
- **Douban**: When you use the extension to query or fill information, it communicates directly with Douban's servers as if you were browsing the site normally.
- **Spotify (Optional)**: If you use features that fetch metadata from Spotify, the extension communicates directly with Spotify's API using the credentials you provide.

We do not act as a middleman in these communications.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify users through the extension's update notes or Chrome Web Store listing.

## Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support link on the Chrome Web Store.
